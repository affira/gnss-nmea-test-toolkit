# Data sources

Every log in `data/` is listed here with its origin and licence. Logs with a licence
we could not confirm are not used. Synthetic logs live in `data/synthetic/`, are
produced by a script in this repo, and say `SYNTHETIC` in their filename and header.

### What the logs contain (checked by hand, 2026-11-12)

- `unicore-um621.log`: 16 epochs with a GPS fix, 4 with no fix (GGA quality 0, 00:07:06–00:07:09 UTC). Fixed
  positions stay within 3.6 m of their mean. The
  header says "Start with fix, loses fix, regains fix." It also contains non-standard
  sentences (`$SNRSTAT`, `$$GYOACC`, `$PDTINFO`) that the parser must skip without
  crashing.
- `ublox-zed-f9p-nmea.log`: all 29 epochs have a GPS fix (quality 1). Positions stay
  within 0.14 m of their mean, so the receiver is effectively static.
- `ublox-neo-m9n-nmea.log`: all 61 epochs have a DGPS/SBAS fix (quality 2). Positions
  stay within 1.7 m of their mean.

### What the real logs cannot show

- **Time to first fix:** all three start with a fix already acquired.
- **UTC day rollover:** none of them crosses midnight.
- **Long outages, or statistically strong CEP:** they cover only 20–61 seconds.

These cases are covered by synthetic logs and by fault injection applied to the real logs.

### Considered and not used

- `quectel-eg25-g.log` (gpsd): useful content, but no per-file licence line, so
  it was left out to keep the rule strict.
