# Data sources

Every log in `data/` is listed here with its origin and licence. Logs with a licence
we could not confirm are not used. Synthetic logs live in `data/synthetic/`, are
produced by a script in this repo, and say `SYNTHETIC` in their filename and header.

## Real recorded logs (`data/real/`)

All three come from the gpsd project's regression test suite.

- Repository: https://gitlab.com/gpsd/gpsd, directory `test/daemon/`
- Retrieved: 2026-11-12, at commit `092e62282f7390840b272692bc258853643b072c` (2026-11-10)
- Licence: **BSD-2-Clause**. Each file's own header states this (see below), and the
  project licence is copied unchanged to `data/real/GPSD-COPYING`.
- The files are copied **unchanged**. The copyright header comments are kept, as the
  BSD-2-Clause licence requires.

Check with `shasum -a 256 data/real/*.log`.

### What the logs contain (checked by hand, 2026-11-12)

- `unicore-um621.log`: 16 epochs with a GPS fix, 4 with no fix (GGA quality 0, 00:07:06–00:07:09 UTC). Fixed
  positions stay within 3.6 m of their mean. The
  header says "Start with fix, loses fix, regains fix." It also contains non-standard
  sentences (`$SNRSTAT`, `$$GYOACC`, `$PDTINFO`) that the parser must skip without
  crashing.
- `ublox-zed-f9p-nmea.log`: all 29 epochs have a GPS fix (quality 1). Positions stay
  within 0.14 m of their mean, so the receiver is effectively static.
- `ublox-neo-m9n-nmea.log`: all 61 epochs have a DGPS/SBAS fix (quality 2). Positions
  stay within 1.7 m of their mean.

### What the real logs cannot show

- **Time to first fix:** all three start with a fix already acquired.
- **UTC day rollover:** none of them crosses midnight.
- **Long outages, or statistically strong CEP:** they cover only 20–61 seconds.

These cases are covered by synthetic logs and by fault injection applied to the real logs.

### Considered and not used

- `quectel-eg25-g.log` (gpsd): useful content, but no per-file licence line, so
  it was left out to keep the rule strict.

## Synthetic logs (`data/synthetic/`)

Generated by `scripts/make_synthetic.py` (code in `src/gnsskit/synthetic.py`). They are
**not real recordings**, and each says so in its filename (`SYNTHETIC-` prefix) and first
header line. `python scripts/make_synthetic.py --check` and the unit tests confirm the
committed files are exactly what the script produces, so they cannot be hand-edited unnoticed.

| File | Scenario | Epochs (1 Hz) | Built-in truth |
|---|---|---|---|
| `SYNTHETIC-cold-start.log` | Cold start | 120 | First 30 epochs without fix, so TTFF = 30 s and availability = 75 % |
| `SYNTHETIC-static-10min.log` | Long static session | 600 | Gaussian noise, sigma 1.0 m per axis, so CEP50 ≈ 1.18 m and CEP95 ≈ 2.45 m in theory |
| `SYNTHETIC-midnight-rollover.log` | Day and year rollover | 121 | 2025-12-31 23:59:00 to 2026-01-01 00:01:00 UTC |

Model and its limits (also stated in each file header):
- Static receiver at the Royal Observatory, Greenwich (51.477928 N, 0.001545 W).
- Horizontal errors are independent from epoch to epoch. Real receiver errors are
  correlated over time, so real CEP from a short log is less reliable than these suggest.
- Fixed sky: 19 satellites (7 GPS, 4 GLONASS, 4 Galileo, 4 BeiDou), all used in the fix.
  The SNR values are made up from elevation, with no physical model behind them.
